The logo is the mark followed by the wordmark, 172 by 36 units: `docs/logo/dark.svg` for dark backgrounds and `docs/logo/light.svg` for light ones. Every variant (the mark alone, side by side and stacked, light and dark, one-color white and black, and a square avatar) is in SVG and PNG in the [brand asset set](https://github.com/LumenWipe/lumenwipe/tree/main/apps/web/brand). All of them, the favicon and the app icons are generated from one source; use the files as they are and do not redraw them.

## The mark and the wordmark

The mark is a rounded square outline with a four-pointed star above a short arc. The wordmark sets "Lumen" in the light or dark neutral and "Wipe" in cyan, in Bricolage Grotesque Bold.

| File                  | For               | Outline and star                   | Arc                                | "Lumen"            | "Wipe"    |
| --------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------ | --------- |
| `docs/logo/dark.svg`  | Dark backgrounds  | `#00B0F0`, the `--accent` token    | `#FAB623`                          | `#FFFFFF`          | `#00B0F0` |
| `docs/logo/light.svg` | Light backgrounds | `#0098D6`, a darker cyan for white | `#E0961A`, a darker gold for white | `#080D0F`, the ink | `#0098D6` |

The arc gold in `docs/logo/dark.svg` is one step off the `--value` token (`#FBB623`). The difference is not visible; it is recorded so nobody "fixes" one to match the other without deciding to.

The wordmark is Bricolage Grotesque Bold outlined to paths, so it looks the same wherever the file is opened, with or without the font. The favicon (`docs/favicon.svg`) is the mark alone on the ink fill, `#080D0F`, the same value as the page background, the app manifest and the browser theme color.

## Clear space and minimum size

The clear space around the logo and its minimum sizes are in the [usage note next to the assets](https://github.com/LumenWipe/lumenwipe/blob/main/apps/web/brand/README.md).

## Backgrounds

- `docs/logo/dark.svg` goes on the page background (`--bg`, `#080D0F`) or any of the dark surfaces (`--surface`, `--surface-raised`, `--surface-sunken`).
- `docs/logo/light.svg` goes on white or a near-white background.
- Neither goes on a photograph, a gradient, a cyan or gold fill, or any background where the outline is hard to see.

## Never

- Recolor the logo, or any part of it. The only single-color versions are the white and black ones in the asset set.
- Swap the colors of "Lumen" and "Wipe", or set the wordmark in another font.
- Stretch, rotate, outline, add a shadow or glow to, or animate the logo.
- Rearrange the mark and the wordmark other than as in the side-by-side and stacked files, or put the wordmark on its own.
- Use the mark's cyan and gold as a reason to use those colors elsewhere. Their meaning in the product is set in [color meaning](/brand/color).
