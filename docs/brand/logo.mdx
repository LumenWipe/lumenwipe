The logo is two files in `docs/logo`: `docs/logo/dark.svg` for dark backgrounds and `docs/logo/light.svg` for light ones. Each is the mark followed by the wordmark, 172 by 36 units. Use the files as they are; do not redraw or re-export them.

## The mark and the wordmark

The mark is a rounded square outline with a four-pointed star above a short arc. The wordmark sets "Lumen" in the light or dark neutral and "Wipe" in cyan, in Bricolage Grotesque Bold.

| File                  | For               | Outline and star                   | Arc                                | "Lumen"               | "Wipe"    |
| --------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------- | --------- |
| `docs/logo/dark.svg`  | Dark backgrounds  | `#00B0F0`, the `--accent` token    | `#FAB623`                          | `#FFFFFF`             | `#00B0F0` |
| `docs/logo/light.svg` | Light backgrounds | `#0098D6`, a darker cyan for white | `#E0961A`, a darker gold for white | `#0B0B14`, near black | `#0098D6` |

The arc gold in `docs/logo/dark.svg` is one step off the `--value` token (`#FBB623`). The difference is not visible; it is recorded so nobody "fixes" one to match the other without deciding to.

The wordmark is live SVG text, not outlined paths. It renders in Bricolage Grotesque only where that font is loaded, as on the docs site and the web app. Where it is not loaded, for example when the file is opened on its own, it falls back to the system sans-serif font. The favicon (`docs/favicon.svg`) is the mark alone on its own dark fill. Unifying the favicon, the app manifest and a downloadable asset set is separate work; this page documents the files as they are.

## Clear space and minimum size

<Note>
  The clear space and minimum sizes below are defined on this page. They were not written down
  before, and existing pages have not been checked against them.
</Note>

- **Clear space.** Keep an empty margin on every side of the logo of at least half the height of the mark. At the native 36px height that is 16px. No text, edge or other graphic enters that margin.
- **Minimum size, full logo.** 24px tall (about 115px wide). Below that the wordmark stops being legible.
- **Minimum size, mark alone.** 16px. Below that the arc and the star merge. Use the mark alone only where the full logo does not fit.

## Backgrounds

- `docs/logo/dark.svg` goes on the page background (`--bg`, `#080D0F`) or any of the dark surfaces (`--surface`, `--surface-raised`, `--surface-sunken`).
- `docs/logo/light.svg` goes on white or a near-white background.
- Neither goes on a photograph, a gradient, a cyan or gold fill, or any background where the outline is hard to see.

## Never

- Recolor the logo, or any part of it, including to a single color.
- Swap the colors of "Lumen" and "Wipe", or set the wordmark in another font.
- Stretch, rotate, outline, add a shadow or glow to, or animate the logo.
- Separate the mark from the wordmark and rearrange them, or put the wordmark on its own.
- Use the mark's cyan and gold as a reason to use those colors elsewhere. Their meaning in the product is set in [color meaning](/brand/color).
