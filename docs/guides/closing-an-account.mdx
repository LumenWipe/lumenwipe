title: "Closing a Stellar account"
description: "What closing an account means, what blocks it, and what you get back."

---

Closing a Stellar account means merging it into another account. The `ACCOUNT_MERGE` operation moves the entire XLM balance to a destination and deletes the source account from the ledger. It cannot be undone.

The network refuses the merge while anything still holds the account open. LumenWipe finds all of it, removes it in the right order, and only then merges.

## What holds an account open

| What                     | Why it blocks the merge                                                              | What LumenWipe does                                                                                            |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trustlines               | Each one is a subentry, and a trustline holding a balance cannot be removed          | Converts the balance to XLM, returns it to the issuer, or sends it where you chose, then removes the trustline |
| Open offers              | Each one is a subentry                                                               | Cancels them                                                                                                   |
| Data entries             | Each one is a subentry                                                               | Deletes them                                                                                                   |
| Extra signers            | They hold reserves, but do not block the merge on their own                          | Removes them first, so one key can authorize the rest of the close                                             |
| Sponsorships you provide | An account that sponsors reserves for others cannot be merged                        | Revokes them when the owner can absorb the reserve, and reports a blocker for the rest                         |
| Claimable balances       | Sponsoring one blocks the merge, being a claimant does not                           | Claims what you can claim, and explains what you cannot                                                        |
| DeFi positions           | The network does not check them, so the merge would strand the funds in the contract | Exits them first. See [exiting protocol positions](/guides/exiting-protocol-positions)                         |

## What you get back

Every Stellar account locks XLM it cannot spend: a 1 XLM minimum balance, plus 0.5 XLM for each trustline, offer, data entry, and extra signer. Closing releases all of it.

- The reserves, and any remaining XLM, arrive at your destination in the merge.
- Balances in other assets are converted to XLM, returned to their issuer, or paid to an account you name. The choice is yours for each asset.
- You pay only the network fees.

<Note>
  An account sitting exactly at its minimum balance cannot pay the fee on its own closing
  transaction. LumenWipe sponsors the fee in that case. The sponsorship can only ever pay fees and
  cannot touch your funds.

Availability by network, as of 2026-10-10: sponsored fees are available on testnet. Mainnet
availability follows the deployment status in the [monitoring plan](/monitoring-plan); where no
fee account is configured, the API reports sponsored closes as unavailable.

</Note>

## Things that stop a close

LumenWipe never builds a transaction it expects to fail, and never skips a step silently. When something cannot be closed safely, you see a blocker with an explanation instead.

- **`AUTH_IMMUTABLE` accounts** can never be merged.
- **A destination that does not exist** must be created and funded first.
- **Claimable balances this account sponsors** stay a blocker until a claimant claims them. The protocol offers no self-service way around it.
- **A protocol that has stopped operating**, such as FxDAO on mainnet (its exit is testnet only), is reported as a blocker rather than attempted.

<Warning>
  Closing an account is irreversible. Check the destination address before you sign, and confirm it
  is a wallet you control.
</Warning>
